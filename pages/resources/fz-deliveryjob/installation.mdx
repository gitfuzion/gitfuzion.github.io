# FzDeliveryJob

## Installation

1. Make sure you have installed all dependencies.

- Utilities
  - [ox_lib](https://github.com/overextended/ox_lib)
- Interactions
  - [qb-target](https://github.com/qbcore-framework/qb-target), [ox_target](https://github.com/overextended/ox_target) or [ox_lib](https://github.com/overextended/ox_lib)
- Database
  - [oxmysql](https://github.com/overextended/oxmysql)

2. Download the resource and place it in your `resources` directory.
3. Add `ensure fz-deliveryjob` to your `server.cfg` file.
4. Configure the resource to your liking.
5. Restart/Start your server.
6. That's it your done!
